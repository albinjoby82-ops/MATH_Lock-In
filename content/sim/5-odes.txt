@sim c-homog-0
Find the general solution of $y''+5y'+6y=0$.
@s Auxiliary equation
$$r^2+5r+6=0$$
@s Solve
$$(r+2)(r+3)=0\ \Rightarrow\ r=-2,\ -3$$
@s Which case?
Two different real roots.
@a $y=C_1e^{-2x}+C_2e^{-3x}$

@sim c-homog-1
Find the general solution of $y''-6y'+9y=0$.
@s Auxiliary equation
$$r^2-6r+9=0$$
@s Solve
$$(r-3)^2=0\ \Rightarrow\ r=3\ \text{(twice)}$$
@s Which case?
Repeated root, so the second part gets an extra $x$.
@a $y=(C_1+C_2x)\,e^{3x}$

@sim c-homog-2
Find the general solution of $y''+4y'+13y=0$.
@s Auxiliary equation
$$r^2+4r+13=0$$
@s Solve with the quadratic formula
$$r=\frac{-4\pm\sqrt{16-52}}{2}=\frac{-4\pm\sqrt{-36}}{2}=\frac{-4\pm6i}{2}=-2\pm3i$$
@s Which case?
Complex: $\alpha=-2$ (real part), $\beta=3$ (the number with $i$).
@a $y=e^{-2x}\left(C_1\cos3x+C_2\sin3x\right)$

@sim c-homog-3
Solve $y''-9y=0$ with $y(0)=2$, $y'(0)=12$.
@s Auxiliary equation
$$r^2-9=0\ \Rightarrow\ r=3,\ -3$$
$$y=C_1e^{3x}+C_2e^{-3x}$$
@s We also need $y'$
$$y'=3C_1e^{3x}-3C_2e^{-3x}$$
@s Use the conditions at $x=0$
$$y(0)=C_1+C_2=2$$
$$y'(0)=3C_1-3C_2=12\ \Rightarrow\ C_1-C_2=4$$
@s Solve
Add the two: $2C_1=6$, so $C_1=3$. Then $C_2=2-3=-1$.
@a $y=3e^{3x}-e^{-3x}$

@sim c-undet-0
Find the general solution of $y''-4y=8x^2$.
@s Step 1: $y_h$
$r^2-4=0\Rightarrow r=\pm2$.
$$y_h=C_1e^{2x}+C_2e^{-2x}$$
@s Step 2: guess $y_p$
Right side is a degree 2 polynomial, so:
$$y_p=Ax^2+Bx+C\qquad y_p''=2A$$
@s Put it in
$$2A-4(Ax^2+Bx+C)=8x^2$$
@s Match coefficients
$x^2$: $\ -4A=8\Rightarrow A=-2$

$x$: $\ -4B=0\Rightarrow B=0$

number: $\ 2A-4C=0\Rightarrow C=\tfrac A2=-1$
$$y_p=-2x^2-1$$
@s Add them
$$y=y_h+y_p$$
@a $y=C_1e^{2x}+C_2e^{-2x}-2x^2-1$

@sim c-undet-1
Find the general solution of $y''+y'-2y=e^{2x}+2x$.
@s Step 1: $y_h$
$r^2+r-2=(r-1)(r+2)=0\Rightarrow r=1,\ -2$.
$$y_h=C_1e^{x}+C_2e^{-2x}$$
@s Step 2: guess $y_p$
Right side is $e^{2x}$ plus a degree 1 polynomial:
$$y_p=De^{2x}+Ax+B$$
No clash: $e^{2x}$ is not in $y_h$.
@s The $e^{2x}$ part
$y=De^{2x}$, $\ y'=2De^{2x}$, $\ y''=4De^{2x}$:
$$4D+2D-2D=4D=1\ \Rightarrow\ D=\tfrac14$$
@s The polynomial part
$y=Ax+B$, $\ y'=A$, $\ y''=0$:
$$0+A-2(Ax+B)=2x$$
$x$: $\ -2A=2\Rightarrow A=-1$

number: $\ A-2B=0\Rightarrow B=\tfrac A2=-\tfrac12$
@s Add everything
$$y=y_h+\tfrac14e^{2x}-x-\tfrac12$$
@a $y=C_1e^{x}+C_2e^{-2x}+\dfrac{e^{2x}}{4}-x-\dfrac12$

@sim c-undet-2
Solve $y''+y'-2y=3e^{x}$ with $y(0)=0$, $y'(0)=4$.
@s Step 1: $y_h$
$r^2+r-2=(r-1)(r+2)=0$:
$$y_h=C_1e^{x}+C_2e^{-2x}$$
@s Step 2: guess $y_p$ (clash!)
Normal guess would be $Ae^{x}$, but $e^{x}$ is already in $y_h$. Multiply by $x$:
$$y_p=Axe^{x}$$
@s Derivatives (product rule)
$$y_p'=A(1+x)e^{x}\qquad y_p''=A(2+x)e^{x}$$
@s Put it in and cancel $e^{x}$
$$A(2+x)+A(1+x)-2Ax=3$$
The $x$ terms cancel: $3A=3$, so $A=1$.
@s Full solution
$$y=C_1e^{x}+C_2e^{-2x}+xe^{x}$$
$$y'=C_1e^{x}-2C_2e^{-2x}+(1+x)e^{x}$$
@s Use the conditions
$y(0)=0$: $\ C_1+C_2=0$

$y'(0)=4$: $\ C_1-2C_2+1=4$

From the first, $C_2=-C_1$. Then $3C_1=3$, so $C_1=1$, $C_2=-1$.
@a $y=e^{x}-e^{-2x}+xe^{x}$
