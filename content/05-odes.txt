@@part Differential equations

@@topic firstorder | First order equations | Sheet 5 · Q1, Q2
@method
**Separable** $y'=g(x)\,h(y)$: put all $y$ on one side, all $x$ on the other, integrate both sides.
$$\int\frac{dy}{h(y)}=\int g(x)\,dx$$

**Linear** $y'+p(x)\,y=q(x)$: multiply by the integrating factor
$$\mu=e^{\int p\,dx}\qquad\text{then}\qquad(\mu y)'=\mu q$$
Integrate, then divide by $\mu$.

**Bernoulli** $y'+p\,y=q\,y^{n}$: substitute $u=y^{1-n}$ to make it linear.

Initial condition: plug it in at the end to find the constant.

@q Sheet 5 · Q1(a)
Solve $y'=ty$, $y(0)=1$.
@s Separate
$$\frac{dy}{y}=t\,dt$$
@s Integrate both sides
$$\ln|y|=\frac{t^2}{2}+c\ \Longrightarrow\ y=Ce^{t^2/2}$$
@s Use y(0) = 1
$$1=Ce^0\ \Longrightarrow\ C=1$$
@a $y=e^{t^2/2}$

@q Sheet 5 · Q1(b)
Solve $y'=1-y$, $y(0)=0$.
@s Separate
$$\frac{dy}{1-y}=dt$$
@s Integrate both sides
$$-\ln|1-y|=t+c\ \Longrightarrow\ 1-y=Ce^{-t}\ \Longrightarrow\ y=1-Ce^{-t}$$
@s Use y(0) = 0
$$0=1-C\ \Longrightarrow\ C=1$$
@a $y=1-e^{-t}$

@q Sheet 5 · Q2(a)
Solve $y'=(1+x)(1+y)$.
@s Separate
$$\frac{dy}{1+y}=(1+x)\,dx$$
@s Integrate both sides
$$\ln|1+y|=x+\frac{x^2}{2}+c$$
@s Solve for y
$$1+y=Ce^{x+x^2/2}$$
@a $y=Ce^{x+x^2/2}-1$

@q Sheet 5 · Q2(b)
Solve $\dfrac{dy}{dt}=e^{-y}t^2$.
@s Separate
Multiply both sides by $e^{y}$:
$$e^{y}\,dy=t^2\,dt$$
@s Integrate both sides
$$e^{y}=\frac{t^3}{3}+C$$
@s Solve for y
Take $\ln$ of both sides.
@a $y=\ln\left(\dfrac{t^3}{3}+C\right)$

@q Sheet 5 · Q2(c)
Solve $y'=1+x^2+y+x^2y$.
@s Factorise the right side
$$1+x^2+y(1+x^2)=(1+x^2)(1+y)$$
Now it is separable.
@s Separate and integrate
$$\frac{dy}{1+y}=(1+x^2)\,dx\ \Longrightarrow\ \ln|1+y|=x+\frac{x^3}{3}+c$$
@a $y=Ce^{x+x^3/3}-1$

@q Sheet 5 · Q2(d)
Solve $\dfrac{dy}{dx}+2xy=xe^{-x^2}$.
@s Type
Linear, with $p=2x$ and $q=xe^{-x^2}$.
@s Integrating factor
$$\mu=e^{\int2x\,dx}=e^{x^2}$$
@s Multiply through
$$\left(e^{x^2}y\right)'=e^{x^2}\cdot xe^{-x^2}=x$$
@s Integrate
$$e^{x^2}y=\frac{x^2}{2}+C$$
@a $y=\left(\dfrac{x^2}{2}+C\right)e^{-x^2}$

@q Sheet 5 · Q2(e)
Solve $y'+\dfrac{2y}{x}=\dfrac{1}{x^2}$.
@s Type
Linear, with $p=\tfrac2x$ and $q=\tfrac1{x^2}$.
@s Integrating factor
$$\mu=e^{\int\frac2x\,dx}=e^{2\ln x}=x^2$$
@s Multiply through
$$\left(x^2y\right)'=x^2\cdot\frac{1}{x^2}=1$$
@s Integrate
$$x^2y=x+C$$
@a $y=\dfrac{x+C}{x^2}=\dfrac1x+\dfrac{C}{x^2}$

@q Sheet 5 · Q2(f)
Solve $y'+xy=xy^{-1}$.
@s Type
Bernoulli with $n=-1$. Substitute $u=y^{1-n}=y^2$.
@s Multiply the equation by y
$$yy'+xy^2=x$$
@s Substitute u = y²
$u'=2yy'$, so $yy'=\tfrac12u'$:
$$\tfrac12u'+xu=x\ \Longrightarrow\ u'+2xu=2x$$
@s Solve this linear equation
$\mu=e^{x^2}$:
$$\left(e^{x^2}u\right)'=2xe^{x^2}\ \Longrightarrow\ e^{x^2}u=e^{x^2}+C\ \Longrightarrow\ u=1+Ce^{-x^2}$$
@s Go back to y
$u=y^2$.
@a $y^2=1+Ce^{-x^2}$, i.e. $y=\pm\sqrt{1+Ce^{-x^2}}$


@@topic homogeneous | Second order homogeneous equations | Sheet 5 · Q3, Q5(a)
@method
For $ay''+by'+cy=0$: replace $y''\to r^2$, $y'\to r$, $y\to1$ and solve the **characteristic equation** $ar^2+br+c=0$.

**Two different real roots** $r_1,r_2$:
$$y=C_1e^{r_1x}+C_2e^{r_2x}$$

**One repeated root** $r$:
$$y=(C_1+C_2x)e^{rx}$$

**Complex roots** $\alpha\pm\beta i$:
$$y=e^{\alpha x}\left(C_1\cos\beta x+C_2\sin\beta x\right)$$

@q Sheet 5 · Q3(a)
Find the general solution of $y''+3y'+2y=0$.
@s Characteristic equation
$$r^2+3r+2=0\ \Longrightarrow\ (r+1)(r+2)=0\ \Longrightarrow\ r=-1,\ -2$$
@s Type
Two different real roots.
@a $y=C_1e^{-x}+C_2e^{-2x}$

@q Sheet 5 · Q3(b)
Find the general solution of $y''-9y'=0$.
@s Characteristic equation
$$r^2-9r=0\ \Longrightarrow\ r(r-9)=0\ \Longrightarrow\ r=0,\ 9$$
@s Type
Two different real roots. $e^{0x}=1$.
@a $y=C_1+C_2e^{9x}$

@q Sheet 5 · Q3(c)
Find the general solution of $y''+10y'+25y=0$.
@s Characteristic equation
$$r^2+10r+25=0\ \Longrightarrow\ (r+5)^2=0\ \Longrightarrow\ r=-5\ \text{(twice)}$$
@s Type
Repeated root: needs the extra $x$.
@a $y=(C_1+C_2x)e^{-5x}$

@q Sheet 5 · Q3(d)
Find the general solution of $y''+2y'+2y=0$.
@s Characteristic equation
$$r^2+2r+2=0\ \Longrightarrow\ r=\frac{-2\pm\sqrt{4-8}}{2}=-1\pm i$$
@s Type
Complex roots with $\alpha=-1$, $\beta=1$.
@a $y=e^{-x}\left(C_1\cos x+C_2\sin x\right)$

@q Sheet 5 · Q5(a)
Solve $y''-16y=0$, $y(0)=1$, $y'(0)=20$.
@s Characteristic equation
$$r^2-16=0\ \Longrightarrow\ r=\pm4$$
$$y=C_1e^{4x}+C_2e^{-4x}$$
@s Differentiate
$$y'=4C_1e^{4x}-4C_2e^{-4x}$$
@s Use the conditions
$$y(0)=C_1+C_2=1$$
$$y'(0)=4C_1-4C_2=20\ \Longrightarrow\ C_1-C_2=5$$
@s Solve
Add: $2C_1=6$, so $C_1=3$, $C_2=-2$.
@a $y=3e^{4x}-2e^{-4x}$


@@topic undetermined | Undetermined coefficients | Sheet 5 · Q4, Q5(b), Q5(c)
@method
$$y=y_h+y_p$$

1. $y_h$: solve the equation with right side $=0$.
2. $y_p$: **guess** something shaped like the right side, with unknown coefficients:

Polynomial of degree $n$ → full polynomial of degree $n$.

$e^{kx}$ → $Ae^{kx}$.

(polynomial)$\,e^{kx}$ → (full polynomial)$\,e^{kx}$.

$\sin kx$ or $\cos kx$ → $A\cos kx+B\sin kx$.

3. **Clash rule:** if your guess is already part of $y_h$, multiply the guess by $x$ (by $x^2$ for a double root).
4. Plug the guess in, match coefficients.
5. Initial conditions go in **last**, into the full $y=y_h+y_p$.

@q Sheet 5 · Q4(a)
Find the general solution of $y''-y=3x^2$.
@s Homogeneous part
$r^2-1=0\Rightarrow r=\pm1$.
$$y_h=C_1e^{x}+C_2e^{-x}$$
@s Guess
Right side is degree 2: $y_p=ax^2+bx+c$. Then $y_p''=2a$.
@s Plug in
$$2a-(ax^2+bx+c)=3x^2$$
@s Match coefficients
$x^2$: $-a=3\Rightarrow a=-3$.

$x$: $-b=0\Rightarrow b=0$.

Constant: $2a-c=0\Rightarrow c=-6$.
@a $y=C_1e^{x}+C_2e^{-x}-3x^2-6$

@q Sheet 5 · Q4(b)
Find the general solution of $y''+4y'-5y=x^2$.
@s Homogeneous part
$r^2+4r-5=(r+5)(r-1)=0$.
$$y_h=C_1e^{x}+C_2e^{-5x}$$
@s Guess
$y_p=ax^2+bx+c$, so $y_p'=2ax+b$, $y_p''=2a$.
@s Plug in
$$2a+4(2ax+b)-5(ax^2+bx+c)=x^2$$
@s Match coefficients
$x^2$: $-5a=1\Rightarrow a=-\tfrac15$.

$x$: $8a-5b=0\Rightarrow b=-\tfrac{8}{25}$.

Constant: $2a+4b-5c=0\Rightarrow c=\tfrac15\left(-\tfrac25-\tfrac{32}{25}\right)=-\tfrac{42}{125}$.
@a $y=C_1e^{x}+C_2e^{-5x}-\dfrac{x^2}{5}-\dfrac{8x}{25}-\dfrac{42}{125}$

@q Sheet 5 · Q4(c)
Find the general solution of $y''-y'-2y=e^x+x$.
@s Homogeneous part
$r^2-r-2=(r-2)(r+1)=0$.
$$y_h=C_1e^{2x}+C_2e^{-x}$$
@s Guess
Two pieces on the right: $y_p=Ae^x+ax+b$. No clash ($e^x$ is not in $y_h$).
@s Plug in the $e^x$ piece
$$Ae^x-Ae^x-2Ae^x=e^x\ \Longrightarrow\ A=-\tfrac12$$
@s Plug in the polynomial piece
$$0-a-2(ax+b)=x$$
$x$: $-2a=1\Rightarrow a=-\tfrac12$. Constant: $-a-2b=0\Rightarrow b=\tfrac14$.
@a $y=C_1e^{2x}+C_2e^{-x}-\dfrac{e^x}{2}-\dfrac x2+\dfrac14$

@q Sheet 5 · Q4(d)
Find the general solution of $y''+6y'+9y=16te^{t}$.
@s Homogeneous part
$r^2+6r+9=(r+3)^2=0$, repeated root $-3$.
$$y_h=(C_1+C_2t)e^{-3t}$$
@s Guess
$y_p=(At+B)e^t$. No clash.
@s Derivatives
$$y_p'=(At+A+B)e^t\qquad y_p''=(At+2A+B)e^t$$
@s Plug in and cancel $e^t$
$$(At+2A+B)+6(At+A+B)+9(At+B)=16t$$
$$16At+(8A+16B)=16t$$
@s Match coefficients
$A=1$. $8+16B=0\Rightarrow B=-\tfrac12$.
@a $y=(C_1+C_2t)e^{-3t}+\left(t-\tfrac12\right)e^{t}$

@q Sheet 5 · Q4(e)
Find the general solution of $y''-2y'+y=x^3+x^2$.
@s Homogeneous part
$(r-1)^2=0$, repeated root $1$.
$$y_h=(C_1+C_2x)e^{x}$$
@s Guess
$y_p=ax^3+bx^2+cx+d$.
$$y_p'=3ax^2+2bx+c\qquad y_p''=6ax+2b$$
@s Plug in and collect
$$ax^3+(b-6a)x^2+(c-4b+6a)x+(d-2c+2b)=x^3+x^2$$
@s Match coefficients
$x^3$: $a=1$.

$x^2$: $b-6=1\Rightarrow b=7$.

$x$: $c-28+6=0\Rightarrow c=22$.

Constant: $d-44+14=0\Rightarrow d=30$.
@a $y=(C_1+C_2x)e^{x}+x^3+7x^2+22x+30$

@q Sheet 5 · Q4(f)
Find the general solution of $y''-4y'+4y=(3x^2+2)e^{x}$.
@s Homogeneous part
$(r-2)^2=0$, repeated root $2$.
$$y_h=(C_1+C_2x)e^{2x}$$
@s Guess
$y_p=u\,e^x$ with $u=ax^2+bx+c$. No clash.
@s Derivatives
$$y_p'=(u'+u)e^x\qquad y_p''=(u''+2u'+u)e^x$$
@s Plug in and cancel $e^x$
$$(u''+2u'+u)-4(u'+u)+4u=u''-2u'+u=3x^2+2$$
@s Put in u
$$2a-2(2ax+b)+ax^2+bx+c=3x^2+2$$
@s Match coefficients
$x^2$: $a=3$.

$x$: $-4a+b=0\Rightarrow b=12$.

Constant: $2a-2b+c=2\Rightarrow c=2-6+24=20$.
@a $y=(C_1+C_2x)e^{2x}+(3x^2+12x+20)e^{x}$

@q Sheet 5 · Q5(b)
Solve $y''-y'-2y=3e^{2x}$, $y(0)=0$, $y'(0)=-2$.
@s Homogeneous part
$(r-2)(r+1)=0$.
$$y_h=C_1e^{2x}+C_2e^{-x}$$
@s Guess (clash!)
$e^{2x}$ is already in $y_h$. Multiply by $x$: $y_p=Axe^{2x}$.
@s Derivatives
$$y_p'=A(1+2x)e^{2x}\qquad y_p''=A(4+4x)e^{2x}$$
@s Plug in and cancel $e^{2x}$
$$A(4+4x)-A(1+2x)-2Ax=3\ \Longrightarrow\ 3A=3\ \Longrightarrow\ A=1$$
@s General solution
$$y=C_1e^{2x}+C_2e^{-x}+xe^{2x}$$
$$y'=2C_1e^{2x}-C_2e^{-x}+(1+2x)e^{2x}$$
@s Use the conditions
$$y(0)=C_1+C_2=0$$
$$y'(0)=2C_1-C_2+1=-2$$
$C_2=-C_1$, so $3C_1=-3$: $C_1=-1$, $C_2=1$.
@a $y=-e^{2x}+e^{-x}+xe^{2x}=(x-1)e^{2x}+e^{-x}$

@q Sheet 5 · Q5(c)
Solve $y''-4y'+3y=4e^{3x}$, $y(0)=-1$, $y'(0)=3$.
@s Homogeneous part
$(r-1)(r-3)=0$.
$$y_h=C_1e^{x}+C_2e^{3x}$$
@s Guess (clash!)
$e^{3x}$ is already in $y_h$: $y_p=Axe^{3x}$.
@s Derivatives
$$y_p'=A(1+3x)e^{3x}\qquad y_p''=A(6+9x)e^{3x}$$
@s Plug in and cancel $e^{3x}$
$$A(6+9x)-4A(1+3x)+3Ax=4\ \Longrightarrow\ 2A=4\ \Longrightarrow\ A=2$$
@s General solution
$$y=C_1e^{x}+C_2e^{3x}+2xe^{3x}$$
$$y'=C_1e^{x}+3C_2e^{3x}+(2+6x)e^{3x}$$
@s Use the conditions
$$y(0)=C_1+C_2=-1$$
$$y'(0)=C_1+3C_2+2=3$$
Subtract: $2C_2+2=4\Rightarrow C_2=1$, $C_1=-2$.
@a $y=-2e^{x}+e^{3x}+2xe^{3x}=(2x+1)e^{3x}-2e^{x}$


@@topic variation | Variation of parameters | Sheet 5 · Q6
@method
For $y''+py'+qy=g(x)$ (make the coefficient of $y''$ equal to **1** first).

1. Get $y_1,y_2$ from the homogeneous equation.
2. Wronskian: $W=y_1y_2'-y_1'y_2$.
3. Compute
$$u_1=-\int\frac{y_2\,g}{W}\,dx\qquad u_2=\int\frac{y_1\,g}{W}\,dx$$
4. $y_p=u_1y_1+u_2y_2$.
5. $y=C_1y_1+C_2y_2+y_p$.

Integration by parts you will need: $\int xe^{kx}dx=\left(\dfrac xk-\dfrac1{k^2}\right)e^{kx}$.

@q Sheet 5 · Q6(a)
Use variation of parameters to solve $y''-9y'+20y=3$.
@s Homogeneous part
$r^2-9r+20=(r-4)(r-5)=0$.
$$y_1=e^{4x}\qquad y_2=e^{5x}$$
@s Wronskian
$$W=e^{4x}\cdot5e^{5x}-4e^{4x}\cdot e^{5x}=e^{9x}$$
@s u₁
$g=3$.
$$u_1=-\int\frac{e^{5x}\cdot3}{e^{9x}}dx=-\int3e^{-4x}dx=\tfrac34e^{-4x}$$
@s u₂
$$u_2=\int\frac{e^{4x}\cdot3}{e^{9x}}dx=\int3e^{-5x}dx=-\tfrac35e^{-5x}$$
@s Particular solution
$$y_p=\tfrac34e^{-4x}e^{4x}-\tfrac35e^{-5x}e^{5x}=\tfrac34-\tfrac35=\tfrac{3}{20}$$
@a $y=C_1e^{4x}+C_2e^{5x}+\dfrac{3}{20}$

@q Sheet 5 · Q6(b)
Use variation of parameters to solve $y''-5y'+6y=5x+1$.
@s Homogeneous part
$(r-2)(r-3)=0$.
$$y_1=e^{2x}\qquad y_2=e^{3x}$$
@s Wronskian
$$W=e^{2x}\cdot3e^{3x}-2e^{2x}\cdot e^{3x}=e^{5x}$$
@s u₁
$$u_1=-\int(5x+1)e^{-2x}dx$$
By parts: $\int(5x+1)e^{-2x}dx=-\tfrac12(5x+1)e^{-2x}-\tfrac54e^{-2x}$. So
$$u_1=\left[\tfrac12(5x+1)+\tfrac54\right]e^{-2x}$$
@s u₂
$$u_2=\int(5x+1)e^{-3x}dx=-\left[\tfrac13(5x+1)+\tfrac59\right]e^{-3x}$$
@s Particular solution
The exponentials cancel:
$$y_p=\tfrac12(5x+1)+\tfrac54-\tfrac13(5x+1)-\tfrac59=\tfrac16(5x+1)+\tfrac{25}{36}$$
$$y_p=\frac{5x}{6}+\frac{31}{36}$$
@a $y=C_1e^{2x}+C_2e^{3x}+\dfrac{5x}{6}+\dfrac{31}{36}$

@q Sheet 5 · Q6(c)
Use variation of parameters to solve $2y''+y'-y=4x$.
@s Make the leading coefficient 1
Divide by 2:
$$y''+\tfrac12y'-\tfrac12y=2x\qquad\text{so }g=2x$$
@s Homogeneous part
$2r^2+r-1=(2r-1)(r+1)=0\Rightarrow r=\tfrac12,\ -1$.
$$y_1=e^{x/2}\qquad y_2=e^{-x}$$
@s Wronskian
$$W=e^{x/2}(-e^{-x})-\tfrac12e^{x/2}e^{-x}=-\tfrac32e^{-x/2}$$
@s u₁
$$u_1=-\int\frac{e^{-x}\cdot2x}{-\frac32e^{-x/2}}dx=\tfrac43\int xe^{-x/2}dx=\tfrac43(-2x-4)e^{-x/2}=-\tfrac83(x+2)e^{-x/2}$$
@s u₂
$$u_2=\int\frac{e^{x/2}\cdot2x}{-\frac32e^{-x/2}}dx=-\tfrac43\int xe^{x}dx=-\tfrac43(x-1)e^{x}$$
@s Particular solution
$$y_p=-\tfrac83(x+2)-\tfrac43(x-1)=-4x-4$$
@a $y=C_1e^{x/2}+C_2e^{-x}-4x-4$


@@topic higher | Higher order equations | Sheet 5 · Q7, Q8
@method
Same ideas as second order, just more roots.

**Characteristic equation:** $y^{(n)}\to r^n$. For a cubic, test $r=\pm1,\pm2,\dots$ (factors of the constant), then divide out.

**Building $y_h$:**

Real root $r$ → $e^{rx}$. Repeated $k$ times → $e^{rx},\ xe^{rx},\dots,x^{k-1}e^{rx}$.

Complex pair $\pm\beta i$ → $\cos\beta x,\ \sin\beta x$. Repeated → also $x\cos\beta x,\ x\sin\beta x$.

**$y_p$:** undetermined coefficients. **Clash rule:** if the guess matches a root of multiplicity $m$, multiply the guess by $x^m$.

**Initial conditions:** $n$ conditions → $n$ equations for the $n$ constants.

@q Sheet 5 · Q7(a)
Solve $y^{(4)}-16y=30\sin t$ with $y(0)=0$, $y'(0)=2$, $y''(\pi)=0$, $y^{(3)}(\pi)=-18$.
@s Characteristic equation
$$r^4-16=(r^2-4)(r^2+4)=0\ \Longrightarrow\ r=\pm2,\ \pm2i$$
$$y_h=C_1e^{2t}+C_2e^{-2t}+C_3\cos2t+C_4\sin2t$$
@s Particular solution
Only even derivatives appear, so $y_p=A\sin t$ is enough. $y_p^{(4)}=A\sin t$.
$$A\sin t-16A\sin t=30\sin t\ \Longrightarrow\ A=-2$$
@s General solution
$$y=C_1e^{2t}+C_2e^{-2t}+C_3\cos2t+C_4\sin2t-2\sin t$$
@s The four conditions
$y(0)=0$: $\ C_1+C_2+C_3=0$.

$y'(0)=2$: $\ 2C_1-2C_2+2C_4-2=2$.

$y''(\pi)=0$: $\ 4C_1e^{2\pi}+4C_2e^{-2\pi}-4C_3=0$.

$y'''(\pi)=-18$: $\ 8C_1e^{2\pi}-8C_2e^{-2\pi}-8C_4-2=-18$.
@s Solve
From the 1st: $C_3=-C_1-C_2$. From the 2nd: $C_4=2-C_1+C_2$.

Into the 3rd: $C_1(e^{2\pi}+1)+C_2(e^{-2\pi}+1)=0$.

Into the 4th: $C_1(e^{2\pi}+1)-C_2(e^{-2\pi}+1)=0$.

Add and subtract: $C_1=0$ and $C_2=0$. Then $C_3=0$, $C_4=2$.
@a $y=2\sin2t-2\sin t$

@q Sheet 5 · Q7(b)
Find the general solution of $y^{(4)}-16y=30\sin2t$.
@s Homogeneous part (same as Q7a)
$$y_h=C_1e^{2t}+C_2e^{-2t}+C_3\cos2t+C_4\sin2t$$
@s Guess (clash!)
$\sin2t$ is already in $y_h$ (root $2i$, once). Multiply by $t$:
$$y_p=t\,(A\cos2t+B\sin2t)$$
@s Shortcut for the derivative
Write $y_p=t\,g$ with $g=A\cos2t+B\sin2t$. Then $g^{(4)}=16g$ and
$$y_p^{(4)}=t\,g^{(4)}+4g'''=16tg+4g'''$$
So $y_p^{(4)}-16y_p=4g'''$.
@s Compute 4g'''
$$g'''=8A\sin2t-8B\cos2t$$
$$4g'''=32A\sin2t-32B\cos2t=30\sin2t$$
@s Match coefficients
$A=\tfrac{30}{32}=\tfrac{15}{16}$, $B=0$.
@a $y=C_1e^{2t}+C_2e^{-2t}+C_3\cos2t+C_4\sin2t+\dfrac{15}{16}\,t\cos2t$

@q Sheet 5 · Q7(c)
Solve $y^{(3)}+y''+y'+y=t$ with $y(0)=y'(0)=y''(0)=0$.
@s Characteristic equation
$$r^3+r^2+r+1=r^2(r+1)+(r+1)=(r+1)(r^2+1)=0\ \Longrightarrow\ r=-1,\ \pm i$$
$$y_h=C_1e^{-t}+C_2\cos t+C_3\sin t$$
@s Particular solution
$y_p=at+b$: $\ 0+0+a+at+b=t\Rightarrow a=1,\ b=-1$.
@s General solution
$$y=C_1e^{-t}+C_2\cos t+C_3\sin t+t-1$$
@s The three conditions
$y(0)=0$: $\ C_1+C_2-1=0$.

$y'(0)=0$: $\ -C_1+C_3+1=0$.

$y''(0)=0$: $\ C_1-C_2=0$.
@s Solve
$C_1=C_2=\tfrac12$, then $C_3=C_1-1=-\tfrac12$.
@a $y=\tfrac12e^{-t}+\tfrac12\cos t-\tfrac12\sin t+t-1$

@q Sheet 5 · Q8(a)
Solve $y'''+4y''-y'-4y=0$.
@s Characteristic equation
$$r^3+4r^2-r-4=r^2(r+4)-(r+4)=(r+4)(r^2-1)=0$$
$$r=-4,\ 1,\ -1$$
@a $y=C_1e^{-4x}+C_2e^{x}+C_3e^{-x}$

@q Sheet 5 · Q8(b)
Solve $y'''+4y''-3y'-18y=0$.
@s Find one root
Try $r=2$: $8+16-6-18=0$. So $(r-2)$ is a factor.
@s Divide it out
$$r^3+4r^2-3r-18=(r-2)(r^2+6r+9)=(r-2)(r+3)^2$$
@s Roots
$r=2$, and $r=-3$ twice.
@a $y=C_1e^{2x}+(C_2+C_3x)e^{-3x}$

@q Sheet 5 · Q8(c)
Solve $y'''+6y''+12y'+8y=0$.
@s Characteristic equation
$$r^3+6r^2+12r+8=(r+2)^3=0$$
$r=-2$ three times.
@a $y=(C_1+C_2x+C_3x^2)e^{-2x}$

@q Sheet 5 · Q8(d)
Solve $y'''+4y''-7y'-10y=0$, $y(0)=-3$, $y'(0)=12$, $y''(0)=-36$.
@s Characteristic equation
Try $r=-1$: $-1+4+7-10=0$.
$$r^3+4r^2-7r-10=(r+1)(r^2+3r-10)=(r+1)(r-2)(r+5)$$
$$y=C_1e^{2x}+C_2e^{-x}+C_3e^{-5x}$$
@s The three conditions
$y(0)$: $\ C_1+C_2+C_3=-3$.

$y'(0)$: $\ 2C_1-C_2-5C_3=12$.

$y''(0)$: $\ 4C_1+C_2+25C_3=-36$.
@s Solve
Add 1st and 2nd: $3C_1-4C_3=9$.

Add 2nd and 3rd: $6C_1+20C_3=-24$.

Double the first of these and subtract: $28C_3=-42\Rightarrow C_3=-\tfrac32$. Then $C_1=1$, $C_2=-\tfrac52$.
@a $y=e^{2x}-\tfrac52e^{-x}-\tfrac32e^{-5x}$

@q Sheet 5 · Q8(e)
Solve $y'''+4y''-7y'-10y=100x^2-64e^{3x}$, $y(0)=-20$, $y'(0)=-\tfrac{29}{5}$, $y''(0)=\tfrac{19}{5}$.
@s Homogeneous part (from Q8d)
$$y_h=C_1e^{2x}+C_2e^{-x}+C_3e^{-5x}$$
@s Guess
$y_p=ax^2+bx+c+De^{3x}$. No clash.
@s Polynomial piece
$y'''=0$, $y''=2a$, $y'=2ax+b$:
$$8a-7(2ax+b)-10(ax^2+bx+c)=100x^2$$
$x^2$: $-10a=100\Rightarrow a=-10$.

$x$: $-14a-10b=0\Rightarrow b=14$.

Constant: $8a-7b-10c=0\Rightarrow c=-\tfrac{89}{5}$.
@s Exponential piece
$$(27+36-21-10)De^{3x}=-64e^{3x}\ \Longrightarrow\ 32D=-64\ \Longrightarrow\ D=-2$$
@s General solution
$$y=C_1e^{2x}+C_2e^{-x}+C_3e^{-5x}-10x^2+14x-\tfrac{89}{5}-2e^{3x}$$
@s The three conditions
$y(0)=-20$: $\ C_1+C_2+C_3=-\tfrac15$.

$y'(0)=-\tfrac{29}{5}$: $\ 2C_1-C_2-5C_3+14-6=-\tfrac{29}{5}\Rightarrow2C_1-C_2-5C_3=-\tfrac{69}{5}$.

$y''(0)=\tfrac{19}{5}$: $\ 4C_1+C_2+25C_3-20-18=\tfrac{19}{5}\Rightarrow4C_1+C_2+25C_3=\tfrac{209}{5}$.
@s Solve
Add 1st and 2nd: $3C_1-4C_3=-14$.

Add 2nd and 3rd: $6C_1+20C_3=28$.

So $28C_3=56\Rightarrow C_3=2$, $C_1=-2$, $C_2=-\tfrac15$.
@a $y=-2e^{2x}-\tfrac15e^{-x}+2e^{-5x}-10x^2+14x-\tfrac{89}{5}-2e^{3x}$

@q Sheet 5 · Q8(f)
Solve $y'''-5y''-y'+5y=10x-63e^{-2x}+29\sin(2x)$.
@s Characteristic equation
$$r^3-5r^2-r+5=r^2(r-5)-(r-5)=(r-5)(r^2-1)=0$$
$$y_h=C_1e^{5x}+C_2e^{x}+C_3e^{-x}$$
@s Guess
$y_p=ax+b+De^{-2x}+E\sin2x+F\cos2x$. No clash.
@s Polynomial piece
$$-a+5(ax+b)=10x\ \Longrightarrow\ a=2,\ b=\tfrac25$$
@s Exponential piece
$$(-8-20+2+5)D=-63\ \Longrightarrow\ -21D=-63\ \Longrightarrow\ D=3$$
@s Trig piece
Put $E\sin2x+F\cos2x$ into the left side and collect:

$\sin2x$: $\ 25E+10F=29$.

$\cos2x$: $\ -10E+25F=0\Rightarrow E=\tfrac52F$.

So $\tfrac{125}{2}F+10F=29\Rightarrow F=\tfrac25$, $E=1$.
@a $y=C_1e^{5x}+C_2e^{x}+C_3e^{-x}+2x+\tfrac25+3e^{-2x}+\sin2x+\tfrac25\cos2x$

@q Sheet 5 · Q8(g)
Solve $y^{(4)}+8y''+16y=0$, $y(0)=-1$, $y'(0)=5$, $y''(0)=-8$, $y'''(0)=-28$.
@s Characteristic equation
$$r^4+8r^2+16=(r^2+4)^2=0\ \Longrightarrow\ r=\pm2i\ \text{(each twice)}$$
$$y=(C_1+C_2x)\cos2x+(C_3+C_4x)\sin2x$$
@s Derivatives at 0
Differentiating and setting $x=0$:
$$y(0)=C_1\qquad y'(0)=C_2+2C_3$$
$$y''(0)=-4C_1+4C_4\qquad y'''(0)=-12C_2-8C_3$$
@s The four conditions
$C_1=-1$.

$-4C_1+4C_4=-8\Rightarrow C_4=-3$.

$C_2+2C_3=5$ and $-12C_2-8C_3=-28$.
@s Solve the last pair
$4\times$ first: $4C_2+8C_3=20$. Add to the second: $-8C_2=-8\Rightarrow C_2=1$, $C_3=2$.
@a $y=(x-1)\cos2x+(2-3x)\sin2x$

@q Sheet 5 · Q8(h)
Solve $y^{(4)}+8y''+16y=64x\sin(2x)$.
@s Homogeneous part (from Q8g)
$$y_h=(C_1+C_2x)\cos2x+(C_3+C_4x)\sin2x$$
@s Guess (double clash!)
Normal guess: $(ax+b)\cos2x+(cx+d)\sin2x$. But $\pm2i$ is a **double** root, so multiply by $x^2$:
$$y_p=x^2\left[(ax+b)\cos2x+(cx+d)\sin2x\right]$$
@s Shortcut for the algebra
$64x\sin2x$ is the imaginary part of $64xe^{2ix}$. Try $Y=u(x)e^{2ix}$. Putting this into $(D^2+4)^2$ shifts $D\to D+2i$:
$$(D^2+4)^2\left[ue^{2ix}\right]=e^{2ix}\left(D^2+4iD\right)^2u=e^{2ix}\left(u''''+8iu'''-16u''\right)$$
@s Solve for u
Take $u=\alpha x^3+\beta x^2$: $u''=6\alpha x+2\beta$, $u'''=6\alpha$, $u''''=0$.
$$48i\alpha-96\alpha x-32\beta=64x$$
$x$: $\alpha=-\tfrac23$. Constant: $\beta=\tfrac{48i\alpha}{32}=-i$.
@s Take the imaginary part
$$Y=\left(-\tfrac23x^3-ix^2\right)(\cos2x+i\sin2x)$$
$$y_p=\text{Im}\,Y=-\tfrac23x^3\sin2x-x^2\cos2x$$
@a $y=(C_1+C_2x)\cos2x+(C_3+C_4x)\sin2x-x^2\cos2x-\tfrac23x^3\sin2x$

@q Sheet 5 · Q8(i)
Solve $y^{(4)}+2y'''-2y'-y=24xe^{-x}+24e^{x}-8\sin x$, $y(0)=-2$, $y'(0)=0$, $y''(0)=6$, $y'''(0)=10$.
@s Characteristic equation
$r=1$ works: $1+2-2-1=0$. $r=-1$ works: $1-2+2-1=0$.
$$r^4+2r^3-2r-1=(r-1)(r+1)^3$$
$$y_h=(C_1+C_2x+C_3x^2)e^{-x}+C_4e^{x}$$
@s Guess (clashes!)
$xe^{-x}$: root $-1$ is triple → $x^3(ax+b)e^{-x}$.

$e^{x}$: root $1$ is single → $cxe^{x}$.

$\sin x$: no clash → $E\sin x+F\cos x$.
@s The $e^{-x}$ piece
Put $y=u\,e^{-x}$; this shifts $D\to D-1$, and $(r-1)(r+1)^3$ becomes $(D-2)D^3$:
$$u''''-2u'''=24x$$
With $u=ax^4+bx^3$: $u'''=24ax+6b$, $u''''=24a$.
$$24a-48ax-12b=24x\ \Longrightarrow\ a=-\tfrac12,\ b=-1$$
@s The $e^{x}$ piece
For a single root the coefficient is $\dfrac{24}{p'(1)}$ where $p'(r)=4r^3+6r^2-2$, so $p'(1)=8$ and $c=3$.
@s The sin piece
With $y=E\sin x+F\cos x$: $y^{(4)}-y=0$, and $2y'''-2y'=-4E\cos x+4F\sin x$.
$$4F\sin x-4E\cos x=-8\sin x\ \Longrightarrow\ F=-2,\ E=0$$
@s General solution
$$y=(C_1+C_2x+C_3x^2)e^{-x}+C_4e^{x}-\left(\tfrac12x^4+x^3\right)e^{-x}+3xe^{x}-2\cos x$$
@s The four conditions
At $x=0$, $y_p$ and its derivatives are $-2,\ 3,\ 8,\ 3$. So:

$C_1+C_4-2=-2$

$-C_1+C_2+C_4+3=0$

$C_1-2C_2+2C_3+C_4+8=6$

$-C_1+3C_2-6C_3+C_4+3=10$
@s Solve
1st: $C_4=-C_1$. 2nd: $C_2=2C_1-3$. 3rd: $2C_3=2C_2-2\Rightarrow C_3=C_2-1$.

4th: $-2C_1+3C_2-6(C_2-1)=7\Rightarrow-2C_1-3C_2=1\Rightarrow-8C_1+9=1$.

$C_1=1,\ C_2=-1,\ C_3=-2,\ C_4=-1$.
@a $y=\left(1-x-2x^2-x^3-\tfrac12x^4\right)e^{-x}+(3x-1)e^{x}-2\cos x$

@q Sheet 5 · Q8(j)
Solve $y^{(4)}+18y''+81y=64x\cos x+108x\cos(3x)$.
@s Characteristic equation
$$r^4+18r^2+81=(r^2+9)^2=0\ \Longrightarrow\ r=\pm3i\ \text{(each twice)}$$
$$y_h=(C_1+C_2x)\cos3x+(C_3+C_4x)\sin3x$$
@s Guess
$64x\cos x$: no clash → $(ax+b)\cos x+(cx+d)\sin x$.

$108x\cos3x$: double clash → $x^2\left[(ex+f)\cos3x+(gx+h)\sin3x\right]$.
@s First piece, using real parts
$64x\cos x=\text{Re}\,(64xe^{ix})$. Try $Y=ue^{ix}$; shifting $D\to D+i$ turns $(D^2+9)^2$ into $(D^2+2iD+8)^2$. For $u=\alpha x+\beta$ only two terms survive:
$$64u+32iu'=64x\ \Longrightarrow\ \alpha=1,\ \beta=-\tfrac i2$$
$$y_{p1}=\text{Re}\left[\left(x-\tfrac i2\right)(\cos x+i\sin x)\right]=x\cos x+\tfrac12\sin x$$
@s Second piece, same idea
$Y=ue^{3ix}$; shifting $D\to D+3i$ turns $(D^2+9)^2$ into $(D^2+6iD)^2$:
$$u''''+12iu'''-36u''=108x$$
With $u=\alpha x^3+\beta x^2$: $72i\alpha-216\alpha x-72\beta=108x\Rightarrow\alpha=-\tfrac12,\ \beta=-\tfrac i2$.
$$y_{p2}=\text{Re}\left[\left(-\tfrac12x^3-\tfrac i2x^2\right)(\cos3x+i\sin3x)\right]=-\tfrac12x^3\cos3x+\tfrac12x^2\sin3x$$
@a $y=(C_1+C_2x)\cos3x+(C_3+C_4x)\sin3x+x\cos x+\tfrac12\sin x-\tfrac12x^3\cos3x+\tfrac12x^2\sin3x$
